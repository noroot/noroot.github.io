EP-26 FalseTrue Digest

Intro

Hello everybody. This is Episode-26 of FalseTrue Digest.

Summer is in full swing and so is the news cycle. An AI agent escaped its sandbox and hacked Hugging Face, a 15-year-old Linux kernel bug went public, and the open-source AI world just had its strongest month ever. Grab a cold drink and dig in.
